---
name: Quote pricing constraint
description: Pricing policy for the Traiteur Amrani online quote flow.
---

Do not show or invent indicative costs until the business supplies approved rates or a pricing formula. The quote form should collect event details for a tailored response.

**Why:** The existing project has no verified prices or calculation rules, and an invented estimate could mislead customers.

**How to apply:** Add price calculations only after the business provides and approves a rate model. Until then, keep the form estimate-free and clear that a tailored quote follows.
